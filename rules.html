COMMUNITY RULES

Simple guidelines so every meet stays fun, respectful and worth coming back to.

01

Respect everyone

Treat drivers, hosts, creators and guests with respect. No harassment, toxicity or discrimination. MidnightClub is for everyone who enjoys the night drive culture.

02

Follow host instructions

During meets and sessions, listen to the hosts. They coordinate the flow, locations and any special rules for that night. Ignoring hosts can result in being asked to leave the session.

03

No griefing or ruining sessions

Do not intentionally disrupt meets — no ramming, blocking, lag switching or other actions that ruin the experience for others. Keep the vibes positive.

04

Respect other crews

When collaborating with crews like Homebound Family or joining servers like Fenrir Yakuza, respect their hierarchy, rules and hosts. Each community has its own leadership.

05

Keep Discord clean

Use channels for their intended purpose. No spam, excessive self-promo, NSFW content outside designated areas, or advertising without permission from hosts.

06

Age & platform rules

Follow Rockstar, FiveM and Discord terms of service. Be mindful of age ratings and platform guidelines when sharing content or inviting others.

07

Have fun, leave no trace of drama

We're here for the cars, the nights and the people. Bring good energy, share the road and help make every session something people want to remember.

Questions about the rules?

ASK A HOST.

If something is unclear or you need clarification, reach out to Bassani_ or BLACKGHOSTz4 on Discord.
