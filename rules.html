MIDNIGHTCLUB / RULES

COMMUNITY RULES

Two sets of guidelines: one for our Discord community and one for every GTA car meet.

01 / COMMUNITY

DISCORD SERVER RULES

Respect the crew. Respect the community.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all members with respect, regardless of rank. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unnecessary arguments.

02

Respect Staff

Follow staff instructions and respect their decisions. If you disagree with a decision, discuss it privately rather than creating public arguments.

03

No Drama or Toxicity

Avoid provoking members, spreading rumors, or creating unnecessary conflicts. Keep personal issues outside the crew.

04

No Spam or Advertising

No message flooding, excessive tagging, or unauthorized promotions. Use channels for their intended purpose.

05

Crew Loyalty

Do not leak private information or conversations. Do not intentionally damage the crew's reputation or create divisions between members.

06

Events & Activities

Respect event hosts, themes, and instructions. No trolling, intentional disruption, or ruining events for others.

07

Voice Channels

No mic spamming, screaming, or intentionally disturbing other members.

⚖
SERVER ENFORCEMENT

DISCIPLINARY SYSTEM

Punishments depend on the severity and frequency of violations.

01

1st OffenseOfficial warning.

02

2nd Offense1-hour to 24-hour timeout.

03

3rd Offense1–3 day timeout or temporary removal.

04

Repeated MisconductKick from the server.

05

Serious ViolationsImmediate temporary or permanent ban.

Immediate Permanent Ban

Harassment, threats, malicious activity, leaking private information, or ban evasion. Staff reserve the right to skip warnings when dealing with serious violations.

FINAL NOTICE

Being part of this crew means respecting its members, rules, and values. Nobody is above the rules, regardless of rank or position.

Respect is expected. Loyalty is valued. Good vibes are mandatory.
02 / IN-GAME EVENTS

CAR MEET RULES

Respect the host. Respect the participants.

01

Follow Host Instructions

All participants must follow host directions and event arrangements.

02

Respect the Theme

Vehicles must match the announced theme and restrictions.

03

No Disruptive Behaviour

No ramming, blocking, fighting, or intentionally disrupting the meet.

04

Respect Others

No harassment, insults, or disrespect towards participants.

05

Keep It Organized

Follow parking arrangements. No reckless driving.

06

No Menu Abuse

No mod menu abuse, trolling, spawning objects, or interfering with other participants. Violations result in immediate removal.

⚖
MEET ENFORCEMENT

DISCIPLINARY SYSTEM

Warnings are issued during the meet before removal.

01

1st WarningVerbal warning.

02

2nd WarningVerbal warning.

03

3rd WarningFinal warning.

04

After 3 WarningsImmediate kick from the meet.

Serious Violations

Serious violations may result in immediate removal without prior warnings.

Respect the rules. No exceptions.
